Lumen: Photo Cleaner, for iPhone

Is anything uploaded? Never your photos or videos: Lumen reads your library on your iPhone. It sends only anonymous usage counts, which you can switch off under “Everything Lumen found”.

Why does Lumen ask for Full Access? To find repeats across your whole library, Lumen has to compare every shot. With limited access it can only look at the photos you picked.

I let photos go. Why hasn’t my storage changed? iOS keeps them in Recently Deleted for 30 days. To get the space back sooner, open Photos, then Collections, Utilities and Recently Deleted, and delete them there. Lumen can’t empty that album for you.

Can I get a photo back? Yes, for 30 days: in Photos, open Collections, Utilities and Recently Deleted, then Recover.

What’s a “smaller copy”? For a big video, Lumen can save the same video in HD with a more efficient format, keeping its date, place and albums, and then let the original go. The copy is checked before anything is removed.
