Lumen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Lumen is made by Ivo Iturrieta. Your photos and videos never leave your iPhone. The only thing Lumen sends is anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off.

What Lumen reads. With your permission, Lumen reads the photos and videos in your library to find similar shots, exact copies, blurry photos, burst extras, screenshots, screen recordings and large files. It does this entirely on your iPhone.

What Lumen keeps. Lumen saves a small index on your iPhone (which photos it has read and what you decided), so it doesn’t have to read everything again. It never leaves your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app.

What Lumen sends. No photo, video or anything about them (names, dates, places, faces or identifiers) ever leaves your device. Lumen has no accounts, advertising or tracking across apps.

To learn what to make better, Lumen sends anonymous usage counts to PostHog, an analytics service: for example that the library was read, a review was opened, or photos were let go, with rounded numbers (“2k–10k photos”, “100–500 MB”) and a random app ID that isn’t linked to you. It doesn’t store your IP address’s location and doesn’t record your screen. Turn the counts off at any time: open “Everything Lumen found” and switch off “Anonymous usage counts”.

Changes to your library. Lumen only removes or changes photos when you choose to, and iOS asks you to confirm each time. Removed items go to Recently Deleted in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Notifications. If you turn on the monthly note, Lumen schedules one local notification a month on your iPhone. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Children. Lumen collects nothing about anyone beyond the anonymous counts above, children included.

Changes. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
